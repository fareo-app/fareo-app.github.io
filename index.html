What Fareo is

Fareo compares estimated fares from taxi and ride-hailing providers. We do not book or operate rides. A ride is a contract between you and the provider.

Fare estimates

Prices are estimates based on typical tariffs unless a price is labelled live. Final prices are set by providers.

Plans and billing

Car hire

Fareo helps you compare car hire offers. Fareo is not a car rental company and does not rent, own or insure vehicles. When you choose an offer, you are taken to the rental company or its booking partner, and your contract is with them.

The rental company sets the price, what is included, the deposit, fuel and mileage rules, insurance and excess, driver age and licence requirements, one-way and late-return fees, and its cancellation and refund policy. Read them before you book. Prices shown in Fareo are estimates unless they are clearly labelled as the company's own live price.

Damage, accidents, fines, tolls, theft, breakdowns, deposits and refunds are handled between you and the rental company under its terms and its insurance. As far as the law allows, Fareo is not responsible for them. Nothing here removes any consumer rights you have by law, including your rights against the rental company and, where applicable, your card provider.

Fareo may receive a commission from the rental company or booking partner when you book. It does not change the price you pay. We show offers in the order the comparison returns them and say so when a result is sponsored.

Booking car hire through Fareo uses bonus from your Fareo plan, in the same way as booking a ride: Fareo shows how much bonus the booking will use before you continue, and you can cancel at that point. Plans with unlimited bonus use none. Bonus is Fareo credit for using Fareo's features. It is not money, cannot be exchanged for cash and has no value outside Fareo.

Fareo charges a booking fee when you continue to the rental company (for example £2.99 in the UK). The fee is shown before you pay, together with the estimated rental price, and is paid by card to Fareo through our payment provider. It is separate from the rental price, which you pay to the rental company. If no booking is made with your booking reference within 7 days, the fee is refunded automatically to the same card. Once your booking is made, the fee is not refunded if you later cancel or change the booking with the rental company, unless the law gives you that right.

You must hold a valid driving licence and meet the rental company's requirements. You are responsible for driving safely and lawfully.

Privacy

Draft summary for discussion. A lawyer must review the final policy before launch.

What we collect

When you create an account: your first and last name, mobile number and email, so we can confirm you are a real person and contact you about your account. Your password and PIN are never stored as they are: only a scrambled, one-way version is kept, in your phone's secure keychain. Face ID and fingerprint are handled by your phone; Fareo never receives your face or fingerprint. A profile photo only if you choose one. Your location only while Fareo is open, to set your pickup. The pickup and destination you search, to show you fares.

Location and map services

When you tap Use my location, your phone shares its GPS position with Fareo. To turn it into an address and postcode, Fareo sends the coordinates to OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) and, in the UK, to postcodes.io. When you type a place, the text and an approximate nearby point are sent to place search services to find matches: Photon (photon.komoot.io), OpenStreetMap (Nominatim), Google Maps Places when it is switched on, and as a last resort your phone's own maps service (Apple or Google). In Car hire, to show rental branches near you, Fareo sends an approximate point (rounded to about 1 km) to OpenStreetMap (Nominatim, and the Overpass map-data service if needed); branch locations, hours and contact details shown in the app come from OpenStreetMap. Road distance and time come from OSRM. If GPS is not available, Fareo can ask ipwho.is for an approximate, city-level position. These services receive the text or coordinates only, never your name or contact details.

Data stored on your phone

Your profile, language, units and your last pickups and destinations are stored on your phone. Whether and when you rated Fareo is stored there too, so we do not ask twice. With Gold, your trip records (provider, route, time, the driver details you add, ratings and notes) are stored encrypted on your phone only; Fareo does not receive them, and deleting your account and data removes them. You can clear the recent places in Settings, and deleting your account in the app removes everything.

Subscriptions and payments

Plans are bought and billed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). To know which plan you have, Fareo uses RevenueCat, which receives your Fareo ID (a random code, not your name) and your purchase status from the store. The car hire booking fee is paid by card through Stripe, our payment provider: Stripe receives your card details and handles the payment, and Fareo only receives the amount, the result and the anonymous booking reference (for example CH-7K2M9QXA), so we can refund the fee if no booking is made. Fareo never sees or stores your full card number. After a ride, the price you paid and your ratings of Fareo and the driver are stored on your phone only.

What we do not do

We only ask for an ID document if something about an account looks unusual, to keep Fareo safe. We never ask for bank details. When you share a fare split with friends, the message goes through your own phone's sharing; Fareo does not receive your contacts. We do not sell your personal data.

Your rights

Under GDPR and UK GDPR you can access, correct, export and delete your data, and object to its use. You can delete your account from Account and subscription.

Affiliate disclosure

When you open a provider from Fareo, we may earn a commission from the provider. It never changes the price you pay.
