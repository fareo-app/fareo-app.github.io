What Fareo is

Fareo compares estimated fares from taxi and ride-hailing providers. We do not book or operate rides. A ride is a contract between you and the provider.

Fare estimates

Prices are estimates based on typical tariffs unless a price is labelled live. Final prices are set by providers.

Plans and billing

The Free plan costs nothing. Paid plans are billed through the App Store or Google Play and renew until you cancel.

Your account

You can delete your account at any time from Account and subscription.

What we collect

Your email when you create an account. Your location only when you tap Use my location. The pickup and destination you search, to show you fares.

Location and map services

When you tap Use my location, your phone shares its GPS position with Fareo. To turn it into an address and postcode, Fareo sends the coordinates to OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) and, in the UK, to postcodes.io. When you type a place, the text and an approximate nearby point are sent to place search services to find matches: Photon (photon.komoot.io), OpenStreetMap (Nominatim), Google Maps Places when it is switched on, and as a last resort your phone's own maps service (Apple or Google). Road distance and time come from OSRM. If GPS is not available, Fareo can ask ipwho.is for an approximate, city-level position. These services receive the text or coordinates only, never your name or contact details.

Data stored on your phone

Your profile, language, units and your last pickups and destinations are stored on your phone. Whether and when you rated Fareo is stored there too, so we do not ask twice. With Gold, your trip records (provider, route, time, the driver details you add, ratings and notes) are stored encrypted on your phone only; Fareo does not receive them, and deleting your account and data removes them. You can clear the recent places in Settings, and deleting your account in the app removes everything.

Subscriptions

Plans are bought and billed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). Fareo never sees your card. To know which plan you have, Fareo uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app ID and your purchase status from the store. After a ride, the price you paid and your ratings of Fareo and the driver are stored on your phone only.

What we do not do

We do not ask for ID, selfies or bank details. We do not sell your personal data.

Your rights

Under GDPR and UK GDPR you can access, correct, export and delete your data, and object to its use. You can delete your account from Account and subscription.

Affiliate disclosure

When you open a provider from Fareo, we may earn a commission from the provider. It never changes the price you pay.

Important notices

Estimates, not quotes. Fares shown are estimates. Final prices are set by each provider.

Independent providers. Rides are provided by independent companies and drivers, not by Fareo. Check the driver, vehicle and plate before you get in, and share your trip with someone you trust.

Not an emergency service. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

AI assistant. Replies in our chat are generated by AI and can be wrong. Never share passwords or card numbers. You can always send a message to a person instead.

Commissions. When you open a provider from Fareo, we may earn a commission. It does not change your price.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to see, correct, export or delete your personal data, object to how it is used, and withdraw consent such as location access at any time. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Your legal consumer rights, including any rights to cancel or to a refund, are not limited by these notices. They depend on your country and on the store you subscribe through.

What we ask of you

Use Fareo honestly: no fake accounts, no automated scraping of our prices, and no abuse of our support team. We may suspend or close accounts that break these rules. Rides you book with a provider follow that provider’s own cancellation and no-show rules, not ours.
